# Our Mission

MALLARD's mission is to bring computational insights into clinical data streams to establish reliable, mechanistically-driven quantitative techniques to advance patient-specific treatment customization and clinical decision support to enhance treatment processes. Our lab works with mathematical and statistical computational models to transform imaging information between timepoints and modalities to extract patient-specific insights about changes in disease state and response assessment during therapy and to establish these quantitative insights as imaging markers to be integrated into clinical prognostic and image-guided treatment tools.
